Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Tangent is a Chrome extension that lets you highlight text on claude.ai and ask short questions in a side bubble, without changing the main conversation. This policy explains what data is involved and where it goes.

Summary

  • Tangent does not run its own servers and does not collect analytics.
  • No third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or remote code are used.
  • Questions and excerpts are sent to claude.ai using your existing signed-in session — the same service you are already using.
  • Bubble state is stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.

Data Tangent handles

When you use Tangent, the extension may process:

  • Text you highlight on claude.ai (the excerpt).
  • Nearby paragraph text used only to resolve references in that excerpt.
  • Questions and follow-ups you type in a bubble.
  • Model replies streamed into that bubble.
  • Preferences you set on the options page (for example, default model and archive-on-close).
  • Bubble metadata needed to restore margin threads (titles, message history, and anchors within a conversation).

Where data goes

claude.ai. To answer a tangent, Tangent creates a side thread and sends your prompt through claude.ai’s web app APIs from the page context. Requests use your existing browser session with Claude. The developer of Tangent does not receive these messages.

Your device. Bubble content and preferences are stored locally with Chrome’s extension storage so threads can survive a refresh or when you return to a conversation. This data stays on your machine unless you sync Chrome data through your own Google account settings.

No Tangent backend. Tangent does not transmit your highlights, questions, or replies to any server operated by the extension author.

Permissions

  • storage — save preferences and soft-persist margin bubbles locally.
  • Content script on https://claude.ai/* — show the highlight UI, gutter, and bubbles on that site only.

Data retention and deletion

Local bubble data remains until you dismiss bubbles, clear extension storage, or uninstall Tangent. Removing the extension deletes its local storage. Content sent to claude.ai is subject to Anthropic’s own retention and privacy terms for your Claude account.

Children

Tangent is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the extension if you are under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the “Last updated” date above will be revised and the new version published at this URL.
